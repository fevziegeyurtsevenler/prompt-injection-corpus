# Ethics & responsible use

This corpus is published for **defensive security research**: red-teaming your own
LLM applications, building and evaluating detections, and teaching. It documents
*techniques and their defenses*, not turnkey attacks.

## Do
- Test systems you own or are explicitly, in writing, authorized to assess.
- Use the material to build detections, harden guardrails and train defenders.
- Report vulnerabilities you find through responsible disclosure.

## Don't
- Use these techniques against systems you do not control.
- Deploy them to cause harm, exfiltrate data, or bypass safety on production
  services you are not authorized to test.

Every technique entry ships with a **defense** on purpose: the goal is resilience,
not exploitation. Unauthorized use may violate computer-misuse laws (e.g. Türkiye
TCK 243–245) and platform terms.
